# Fixed Horizon Counterfactual

- this is a research hypothesis, not the current strategy
- comparison baseline: current strategy with terminal exits
- comparison counterfactual: future-only / no-terminal fixed-horizon subset
- observed pattern: future_clean is better at 6h and 24h, while terminal_clean is weaker at 24h
- implication: current edge may be carried disproportionately by future-only survivors rather than the full exit-enabled strategy
- conclusion: current strategy is not proven; at most, a fixed-horizon hypothesis is suggested
